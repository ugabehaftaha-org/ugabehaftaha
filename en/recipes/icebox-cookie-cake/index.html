Icebox Cookie Cake

In memory of Ziv

Ziv was a logistics supervisor in Golani’s 51st Battalion, where he served for 18 years. On October 7 he was called to the base at Kissufim and was ambushed by terrorists on the way. He did everything quietly and humbly, helped people before they asked, and gave every soldier personal attention. His wife says: “He was an amazing husband, the man I always dreamed of. Supportive, loving, accepting. He was the anchor for me and for Mia. A perfect dad who loved Mia so much and was so proud of her.”

Ingredients

  • 1 package tea biscuits
  • 1 container heavy cream
  • 1 cup soft white cheese
  • 1 packet instant vanilla pudding
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 cup coffee (1 tsp instant coffee in boiling water)

Instructions

  1. Whip the cream, pudding, sugar and milk to a smooth cream, then fold in the cheese.
  2. Dip the biscuits in coffee for a few seconds and lay a layer in the pan.
  3. Spread a third of the cream, and make two more layers of biscuits and cream.
  4. Chill a few hours. Enjoy.
