Whole-Wheat Eshel Donut Bites

A recipe by Keren Agam

Sugar-free

Ingredients

  • 350 g whole-wheat or all-purpose flour + 2 tsp baking powder (or a small bag of self-rising flour)
  • 2 cups eshel (Israeli cultured milk; buttermilk works)
  • 4 packets vanilla sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • Oil for frying

Instructions

  1. Heat deep oil in a medium pot and line a plate with paper towels.
  2. Mix everything in a bowl until smooth.
  3. When the oil is hot, drop in teaspoonfuls of batter, using a second spoon to slide them in.
  4. Fry 2–3 minutes per side, drain and dust with powdered sugar.
  5. Try not to eat them all yourself.

These are only lightly sweet. The powdered sugar does most of the work.
