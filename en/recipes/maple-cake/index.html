Maple Cake

In memory of Omri Niv Feierstein

Omri Niv Feierstein was a sweet, kind-hearted boy, smart and funny, with bright eyes. A wonderful brother to his sisters, son and friend. He went to Borochov school and Kalai High School in Givatayim, was a great basketball player and a counselor in the Working and Studying Youth movement. Omri fell on October 7, 2023, in the battle at the Zikim base. He and his company fought bravely, stopped the terrorists from breaking in and protected about 120 recruits and civilians. Maple cake was Omri’s favorite.

Ingredients

  • 1 container sour cream
  • 3 eggs
  • 1½ cups self-rising flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 3 packets vanilla sugar
  • ½ cup canola oil
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • ½ cup broken nuts

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (180°C).
  2. Mix all the ingredients, adding the flour last.
  3. Bake 40 minutes. Top with a little maple syrup and nuts if you like.
