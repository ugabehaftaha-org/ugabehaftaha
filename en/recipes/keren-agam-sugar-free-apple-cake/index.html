Sugar-Free Apple Cake

A recipe by Keren Agam

Sugar-free

Ingredients

  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup sugar substitute (Keren used Sweetango)
  • ¾ cup (150 g) canola oil
  • 1 cup (240 ml) milk (Keren used goat milk; any milk works)
  • 2 tsp good vanilla extract or paste
  • 2.5 cups (350 g) all-purpose flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • For the apples: 3–4 medium apples, peeled and diced
  • 2 tsp cinnamon
  • 2 tbsp sugar substitute
  • A handful of sliced almonds
  • To finish: sugar-free powdered sugar (optional)

Instructions

  1. Toss the diced apples with 2 tbsp sugar substitute and the cinnamon. Set aside.
  2. Heat the oven to 350°F (180°C) and grease 2 pans.
  3. Whisk the eggs and sugar substitute until fluffy. Slowly drizzle in the oil while whisking, then add the milk and vanilla.
  4. Add the baking powder and flour and whisk until smooth.
  5. Pour a quarter of the batter into each pan and top with a quarter of the apples. Repeat with the rest.
  6. Sprinkle with almonds and bake 35–40 minutes, until a toothpick comes out with moist crumbs.
  7. Let cool a little and dust with sugar-free powdered sugar.
