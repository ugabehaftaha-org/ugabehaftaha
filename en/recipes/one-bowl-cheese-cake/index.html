One-Bowl Cheese Cake

Recipe by Lilach

Ingredients

  • 500 g soft white cheese
  • 1 cup sugar
  • ½ cup oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract or 1 packet vanilla sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 2 cups self-rising flour (or all-purpose + 2 tsp baking powder)

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 340°F (170°C) and grease 2 loaf pans.
  2. Mix the cheese, sugar, vanilla and oil. Add the eggs one at a time.
  3. Fold in the flour just until combined.
  4. Divide between the pans (about ¾ full) and bake about 35 minutes, until golden and a skewer comes out with moist crumbs.

Upgrade: scatter a few frozen berries on top before baking.
