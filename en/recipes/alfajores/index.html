Alfajores

In memory of Kim

Kim was an officer and commander in the Border Police patrol unit. She stopped attacks and saved many lives. She was known as a lioness, with fire in her eyes, and liked to remind her trainees: “The animal in me is only sleeping, it isn’t dead.” Kim was murdered at the Nova festival on October 7, 2023, with her friend Raz Mizrahi. She was 22. Since childhood she loved making alfajores, and everyone always asked for her recipe. Her cousin Meitar says: “While Kim was still listed as missing, we baked alfajores from her recipe so that when she came back, she could eat what she loved most. For us, it was hope.”

Ingredients

  • Dough: 200 g soft butter
  • 100 g powdered sugar
  • 4 large egg yolks
  • 1⅓ cups cornstarch
  • 1⅓ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ¼ tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp grated lemon zest
  • Filling: 1 cup dulce de leche
  • Coating: 1 cup shredded coconut

Instructions

  1. Beat the butter and powdered sugar until smooth and fluffy, then add the yolks one at a time.
  2. Sift in the remaining dry ingredients and mix just until a dough forms. Chill 1–2 hours.
  3. Heat the oven to 320°F (160°C). Roll to ¼ inch, cut circles and bake about 10 minutes, until the edges just start to turn golden.
  4. Cool, spread dulce de leche on half the cookies and top with the rest.
  5. Roll the edges in coconut. (If you love lots of filling, like Kimmy did, spread it around the sides too.)
