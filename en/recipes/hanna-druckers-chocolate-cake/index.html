Hanna Drucker’s Chocolate Cake

Hanna with volunteer Keren Arbel

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 3 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 100 g dark chocolate
  • 1½ cups sugar
  • ½ cup milk
  • 1½ cups self-rising flour
  • 1 stick (250 g) margarine

Instructions

  1. In a small pot, cook the milk, cocoa, chocolate and ½ cup sugar. Remove from heat, add the margarine and let cool.
  2. Separate the eggs and stir the yolks into the chocolate mixture. Set aside ½ cup for the glaze.
  3. Whip the whites with the remaining cup of sugar.
  4. Stir the flour into the chocolate mixture until smooth, then fold in the whites.
  5. Bake in a 9.5-inch pan at 340°F (170°C). Pour the glaze over the cake as soon as it comes out. Good luck!

The story behind the recipe

Keren writes: Hanna and I met about four years ago and found out we lived in the same neighborhood, a few buildings apart. We’ve been inseparable ever since, even after Hanna moved. She knows my parents, I know some of her kids and grandkids, and I still get excited every time I visit. We sit and chat like two friends. Age is just a number for us, and what we have goes far beyond the Surprise Cake project. I love you, Hanna.
