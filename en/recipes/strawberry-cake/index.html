Strawberry Cake

In memory of Eden

Eden grew up in Beit Hashmonai and excelled at school. He served in the Artillery Corps and then studied electrical and electronics engineering at HIT in Holon. His friends say he was kind, modest and always smiling. He loved life, time with family and friends, and always helped everyone. Eden was murdered at the Nova festival on October 7, 2023, at 27. He used to ask his mother for strawberry cake, his favorite, and she would make it just for him.

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 3 tbsp sugar
  • Vanilla sugar
  • ½ packet baking powder
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 package strawberries and a little powdered sugar
  • 2 packets instant strawberry or vanilla pudding
  • 2 containers heavy cream
  • 1 packet strawberry Jell-O
  • 2 cups hot water

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 340°F (170°C).
  2. Whip the eggs with the sugar, vanilla sugar, baking powder, oil and one packet of pudding, and bake.
  3. While it bakes, halve the strawberries and toss with powdered sugar.
  4. Whip the cream with the second pudding packet and the milk. Make the Jell-O according to the package.
  5. Spread the cream over the cake, arrange the strawberries and pour the Jell-O on top. Cover and chill.
