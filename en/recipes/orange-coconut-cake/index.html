Orange Coconut Cake

Recipe by Shachar

Sugar-free

Ingredients

  • 1 egg
  • ⅓ cup sweetener (monk fruit or Sweetango)
  • ½ cup oil
  • ½ cup orange juice (store-bought is fine)
  • ½ cup lukewarm water
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ¾ cup shredded coconut

Instructions

  1. Mix the egg, sweetener, oil, orange juice, water and vanilla.
  2. Add the flour, baking powder and coconut and stir.
  3. Bake at 350°F (180°C) for about 30 minutes.

Upgrade: 10 minutes after the cake comes out, pour a little orange juice over it.
