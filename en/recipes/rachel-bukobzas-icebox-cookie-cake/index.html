Rachel Bukobza’s Icebox Cookie Cake

Thanks to volunteer Ilanit Saban

Ingredients

  • 2 packages tea biscuits (Petit Beurre)
  • 2 cups milk for dipping
  • 2 containers heavy cream (500 ml)
  • 1 packet instant vanilla pudding
  • 1 cup milk

Instructions

  1. Whip the cream with the cup of milk and the pudding until thick.
  2. Dip biscuits in milk and lay a layer in the pan. Spread with cream.
  3. Keep layering, finishing with cream. Dust with cocoa or grated chocolate if you like.
  4. Chill at least 4–6 hours, until set. Enjoy.
