Nurit Ohayon’s Homemade Croissants

Nurit with volunteer Soli Gofin

Ingredients

  • 4 cups flour (measured with a disposable cup)
  • 1 block margarine
  • 2 eggs
  • 3 tbsp sugar
  • 1 cup milk (disposable cup)
  • 1 tbsp dry yeast
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • Chocolate spread or chocolate pieces for filling
  • Egg and sesame seeds for topping
  • Syrup: 1½ cups sugar, 1 cup water and a little lemon

Instructions

  1. Beat the eggs with the sugar, then gradually add the milk, flour, yeast, oil and baking powder. Knead on the counter.
  2. Divide the margarine into 3. Spread a third over the dough, fold in thirds like a letter and chill 1½ hours.
  3. Repeat the rolling, spreading and folding twice more, chilling 1½ hours each time.
  4. Divide into 4, roll out, cut into triangles, put chocolate in the center and roll up.
  5. Brush with egg, sprinkle with sesame and bake until browned, first on the bottom rack, then the middle.
  6. Boil the sugar, water and lemon to a thick syrup and brush it over the croissants the moment they come out.

Finger-licking good, guaranteed.
