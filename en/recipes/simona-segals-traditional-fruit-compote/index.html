Simona Segal’s Traditional Fruit Compote

Simona with volunteer Ilana Milishtein

Ingredients

  • 4 apples (Grand or Gala), peeled and quartered
  • 4 pears, cut up
  • 6–8 fresh or dried plums
  • A handful of dried apricots
  • A handful of prunes
  • 2–3 cinnamon sticks
  • 3–4 cloves (optional)
  • 1 strip lemon peel (no white pith)
  • 2–3 tbsp sugar, to taste
  • 1.5–2 quarts water

Instructions

  1. Put all the fruit in a pot and add the water, cinnamon, cloves and lemon peel.
  2. Bring to a boil, lower to medium-low and simmer 30–40 minutes, until the fruit is soft but holding its shape.
  3. Taste and add sugar as needed.
  4. Cool completely and refrigerate for a few hours.

Simona’s tips: with dried fruit only, 25–30 minutes is enough. A few raisins add natural sweetness, and a star anise adds aroma. Best served with plenty of the cold liquid and a few pieces of fruit in every glass.
