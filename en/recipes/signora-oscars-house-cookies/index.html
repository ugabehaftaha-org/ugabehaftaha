Signora Oscar’s House Cookies

Signora with volunteer Pnina Biton

Ingredients

  • 6 eggs
  • 1¼ cups oil
  • 1¼ cups sugar
  • 1 packet vanilla sugar
  • 2.2 lb (1 kg) flour
  • 3 packets baking powder

Instructions

  1. Crack the eggs into a deep bowl and mix while adding the sugar.
  2. Add the baking powder, vanilla and oil and keep mixing.
  3. Add the flour, holding back about a cup, and knead by hand until the dough is soft and shiny.
  4. Shape into rings, like ka’ak, and bake at 350°F (180°C) until golden.
