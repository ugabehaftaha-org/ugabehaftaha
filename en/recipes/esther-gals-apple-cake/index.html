Esther Gal’s Apple Cake

Thanks to volunteer Esti Avni

Ingredients

  • Bowl 1: 4 eggs
  • 1½ cups sugar
  • ½ cup oil
  • Grated lemon zest
  • 1½ cups flour
  • 1 packet baking powder
  • Bowl 2: 4 apples, diced (ideally 2 sweet, 2 tart), soaked in a cup of sweet wine for at least an hour, then mashed with a fork
  • 10 walnuts, chopped
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp ground cloves (optional)
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Mix the contents of bowl 2.
  2. Fold it into bowl 1.
  3. Bake in a rectangular pan for 20 minutes at 320°F (160°C), then 20 more at 300°F (150°C). Enjoy.
