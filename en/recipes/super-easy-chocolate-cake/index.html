Super Easy Chocolate Cake

Recipe by Bar

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 1½ cups sugar
  • 1 packet vanilla sugar
  • 2 cups water
  • 4 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 2 cups self-rising flour
  • 1 cup oil

Instructions

  1. Mix all the ingredients in a bowl, in order.
  2. Bake at 300°F (150°C) for about 30–40 minutes.

Upgrade: melt 100 g dark chocolate with 250 ml heavy cream and pour it over the cake.
