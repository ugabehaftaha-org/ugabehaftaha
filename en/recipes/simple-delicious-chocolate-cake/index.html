Simple, Delicious Chocolate Cake

Recipe by Lilachush

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 cup canola or sunflower oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract or 1 packet vanilla sugar
  • 1 cup boiling water
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 packet baking powder
  • 3 tbsp cocoa powder
  • Glaze: 100 g dark or milk chocolate
  • 100 ml heavy cream

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 340°F (170°C) and grease 2 loaf pans.
  2. Mix the sugar, eggs, oil and vanilla. Add the boiling water.
  3. Add the flour, baking powder and cocoa and mix until smooth.
  4. Divide between the pans and bake about 35 minutes, until a skewer comes out with moist crumbs.
  5. Melt the chocolate and cream in the microwave and pour over the cakes.

Upgrade: stir a cup of chopped chocolate or chocolate chips into the batter.
