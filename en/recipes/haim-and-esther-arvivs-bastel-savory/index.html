Haim & Esther Arviv’s Bastel (Savory)

Sugar-free

Haim and Esther Arviv · Thanks to volunteer Tiki Gamliel

Haim comes from the Tripolitan Jewish community, and he shared one of its savory recipes with us.

Ingredients

  • 6 large potatoes
  • 3 tbsp matzo meal
  • 2 eggs (yolks for the dough, whites for frying)
  • 1 onion
  • 2.2 lb (1 kg) ground chicken breast or ground beef
  • 1 bunch parsley
  • Salt and black pepper

Instructions

  1. Boil the potatoes in their skins (to keep the starch). Once soft, peel and mash.
  2. Mix in salt, pepper, matzo meal and the 2 yolks.
  3. Meanwhile, sauté a chopped onion, add the meat, salt, pepper and parsley, and cook until the liquid is gone.
  4. Flatten 2 tbsp of mash in your palm, add a spoonful of filling in the center, wrap it up and shape an oval patty.
  5. Dip each bastel in beaten egg whites and fry until browned.

Bastel is usually made for the holidays. Enjoy!
