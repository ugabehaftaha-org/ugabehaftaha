Chocolate Banana Hazelnut Balls

A recipe by Keren Agam

Sugar-free

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe bananas
  • 120 g shredded coconut (1¼ cups)
  • 20 g cocoa powder (2 tbsp)
  • ¼ cup chopped hazelnuts
  • To coat: ¼ cup shredded coconut

Instructions

  1. Mash the bananas well in a bowl.
  2. Add the cocoa, coconut and hazelnuts and mix until even.
  3. Roll into balls and coat in coconut.
  4. Keep refrigerated.

Swap the hazelnuts for any nut, the same amount of crushed cookies, or leave them out. You can coat the balls in cocoa instead of coconut.
