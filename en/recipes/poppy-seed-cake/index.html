Poppy Seed Cake

Recipe by Ruchale

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • ½ cup sugar
  • 100 g poppy seeds
  • 1 packet baking powder
  • 1 cup milk
  • ¾ cup oil
  • 3 eggs

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 340°F (170°C).
  2. Mix the dry ingredients: flour, poppy seeds and baking powder.
  3. In another bowl, beat the eggs and sugar, then add the oil and milk.
  4. Combine, pour into a loaf pan (enough for two) and bake 40 minutes.
