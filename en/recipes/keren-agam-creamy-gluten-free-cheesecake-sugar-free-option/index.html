Creamy Gluten-Free Cheesecake, Sugar-Free Option

A recipe by Keren Agam

Sugar-free

Ingredients

  • 5 large eggs
  • 1 cup (200 g) Sweetango sweetener, or regular sugar
  • 1 cup (250 g) soft white cheese, 9%
  • 500 g (2.5 containers) Tzuriel Farm goat cream cheese
  • 1 container (150 g) Tzuriel Farm goat yogurt
  • 1 tbsp vanilla paste or extract
  • 6 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1 cup boiling water (for the outer pan, not the cake!)
  • To finish: powdered sugar

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 320°F (160°C) and lightly grease a pan.
  2. Whip the eggs with the sweetener for 6–7 minutes, until very pale and fluffy. Don’t skip this.
  3. Mix in the yogurt, white cheese and goat cream cheese. Gently fold in the vanilla and cornstarch.
  4. Pour into the pan and wrap the bottom and sides in foil.
  5. Set it inside a larger pan, place in the oven and pour boiling water into the outer pan.
  6. Bake 40–45 minutes, until lightly browned on top and just wobbly in the center.
  7. Turn off the oven, prop the door open slightly and let the cake cool inside for 30–40 minutes. Cool fully, then refrigerate.
