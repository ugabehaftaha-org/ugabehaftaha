Insa Epstein’s Marble Sour Cream Cake

Thanks to volunteer Diana Korol

Ingredients

  • 3 eggs
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 packet vanilla sugar
  • 1 container sour cream (200 ml)
  • ½ cup oil
  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 packet baking powder
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1–2 tbsp milk (to mix with the cocoa)

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 340°F (170°C).
  2. Beat the eggs, sugar and vanilla sugar for about 3 minutes. Add the oil and sour cream.
  3. Add the flour and baking powder and mix just until smooth.
  4. Divide between two bowls. Stir the cocoa and milk into one.
  5. Pour the two batters alternately into a large loaf pan or 9.5-inch round pan, and swirl with a toothpick for a marble look.
  6. Bake 40–50 minutes. Dust with powdered sugar once cool, if you like.

The story behind the recipe

When her kids were small, this cake was a family ritual. Everyone gathered in the kitchen: one cracked eggs, one stirred, another added the cocoa for the two-tone cake they loved. Today, past 90, Insa no longer bakes alone. Her beloved caregiver stirs and pours beside her, but her eyes still sparkle the way they always did. Every slice tells a story, and every bite brings back a small moment from the past.
