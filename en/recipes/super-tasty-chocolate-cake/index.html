Super Tasty Chocolate Cake

Recipe by Shachar

Sugar-free

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • ½ cup oil
  • ¾ cup sweetener (monk fruit, stevia or Sweetango)
  • ¾ cup lukewarm water
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • 1½ tbsp cocoa powder

Instructions

  1. Mix the eggs, oil, sweetener and water.
  2. Add the flour, baking powder, baking soda and cocoa and stir.
  3. Pour into a greased loaf pan and bake at 350°F (180°C).
