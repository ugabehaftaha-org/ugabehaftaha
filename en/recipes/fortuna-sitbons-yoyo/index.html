Fortuna Sitbon’s Yoyo

Fortuna with volunteer Lee Uria Har-Zahav

Ingredients

  • 6 eggs
  • 1 cup oil
  • 3 packets baking powder
  • 3 packets vanilla sugar
  • 1 whole orange, ground with peel and juice
  • 2.2 lb (1 kg) flour
  • 2.2 lb (1 kg) sugar + 1 cup

Instructions

  1. Crack the eggs into a mixer, slowly add 1 cup sugar and whip well.
  2. Add the ground orange, then slowly the oil, and keep whipping.
  3. Add the flour gradually. Near the end, mix a little flour with the baking powder and vanilla and add it. Hold back some flour for when the dough is soft and sticky.
  4. Refrigerate overnight.
  5. Heat deep oil, shape rings from the dough and fry.
  6. Melt 2.2 lb sugar in water with lemon juice. When the syrup thickens, turn off the heat and dip the fried yoyos in it.

Perfect for Hanukkah and Purim.
