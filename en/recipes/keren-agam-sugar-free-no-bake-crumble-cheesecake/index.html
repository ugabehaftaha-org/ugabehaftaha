Sugar-Free No-Bake Crumble Cheesecake

A recipe by Keren Agam

Sugar-free

Ingredients

  • Crumble: 1.5 cups (210 g) all-purpose flour
  • 150 g cold butter, cubed
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • ½ cup (60 g) Sweetango powdered sweetener
  • 2 egg yolks
  • Filling: 2 containers (500 g) heavy cream, 38%
  • 1 container (200 g) sour cream, 15% or richer
  • ½ cup + 2 tbsp (130 g) Sweetango or another sweetener
  • ½ packet (40 g) instant vanilla pudding
  • 500 g soft white cheese, 5% or richer
  • ½ container (125 g) full-fat cream cheese

Instructions

  1. Line a 9.5-inch (24 cm) round pan and a large sheet pan with parchment. Heat the oven to 350°F (180°C).
  2. In a food processor, pulse the flour, baking powder and sweetener. Add the butter, pulse briefly, then the yolks, until crumbs form.
  3. Press about ⅔ of the crumbs into the round pan and scatter the rest on the sheet pan.
  4. Bake both 15–20 minutes until golden, then cool completely.
  5. Whip the cream, sweetener and pudding to stiff peaks. On low speed, mix in the sour cream, white cheese and cream cheese.
  6. Spread the filling over the base, top with the remaining crumbs and chill at least 8 hours, ideally overnight.
  7. Keeps up to 4 days in the fridge, well wrapped.
