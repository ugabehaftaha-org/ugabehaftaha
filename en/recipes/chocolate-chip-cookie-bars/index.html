Chocolate Chip Cookie Bars

In memory of Omer

Omer had finished his army service 11 months earlier and was starting his life. He had just taken his college entrance exam, found a wonderful girlfriend and booked a ticket for the big trip to South America. On that Black Sabbath he got his call-up order and went to reserve duty for the first time, full of motivation and love for the country. Omer fell at Kibbutz Nirim from mortar fire on October 14, 2023, at 22. His sister Shelly says: “One day I sent him a video of this recipe and he asked me to make it. He took it to the base and shared it with his friends, and everyone loved it. Since then, it’s the cake Omer loves.”

Ingredients

  • 225 g soft butter
  • 150 g brown sugar
  • 75 g white sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 270 g all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 200 g milk chocolate chips
  • 100 g white chocolate chips
  • 100 g melted Lotus Biscoff spread

Instructions

  1. With the paddle attachment, beat the butter, brown sugar and white sugar until smooth.
  2. Add the eggs, then all the dry ingredients, and finally the chocolate.
  3. Spread in a pan, make dents with a spoon and fill each one with Biscoff spread.
  4. Bake at 320°F (160°C, convection) for about 20–25 minutes.
