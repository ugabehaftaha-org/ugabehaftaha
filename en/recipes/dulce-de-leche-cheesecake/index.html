Dulce de Leche Cheesecake

Recipe by Atara

Ingredients

  • Base: 150 g ground biscuits
  • 100 g melted butter
  • 3 tbsp dulce de leche
  • Batter: 750 g soft white cheese, 5%
  • 2 containers sour cream, 15%
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 3 tbsp flour
  • 3 tbsp dulce de leche
  • Topping: 1 container heavy cream, 38%
  • ½ cup milk
  • 3 tbsp vanilla pudding mix

Instructions

  1. Mix the biscuits and butter and press into a 10-inch (26 cm) pan. Spread 3 tbsp warmed dulce de leche on top.
  2. Mix the cheese, sour cream, eggs, sugar and flour. Spread half over the base.
  3. Stir 3 tbsp dulce de leche into the other half and add it on top.
  4. Bake 1 hour at 320°F (160°C), with a pan of water on the oven floor.
  5. Chill overnight. Whip the cream, milk and pudding mix and pipe on top.
